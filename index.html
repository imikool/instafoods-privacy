InstaFoods Privacy Policy

Scope

InstaFoods is part of the Instafresh family. This policy covers the Android testing build of InstaFoods for Pakistan, version 0.1.3, build 5. The app lets you browse sample restaurants and simulate food orders in Lahore and Karachi.

Location and addresses

You select Lahore or Karachi and can type a sample delivery address. The app stores your selection and the address on your device to display them in checkout and order history. It does not request location permission, read GPS coordinates, or track your location in the foreground or background. Restaurant distances and delivery estimates are sample values.

Profile and account information

If you enter a display name, the app saves it locally for the demo profile. It also saves your language, city, delivery or pickup preference, favourites and cart. You can use a sample name and address. This build has no shared sign-in and does not create a customer account on a server.

Order history, delivery and loyalty

Simulated order records include the restaurant, items, quantities, prices, service fee, selected demo payment method, order identifier, time, address, order type and status. Local records also include simulated rider assignments, progress, alert records and demo settings. The app uses completed orders and locally stored point values to show loyalty points, tier and points history. Anyone using the unlocked app can view these demo records and open its operations screens.

External services and sharing

In this testing build, InstaFoods does not collect or share profile, address, order, loyalty or other demo data off-device. There is no live ordering, payment processing, rider dispatch, notification sending, advertising or analytics service. JazzCash, EasyPaisa and card payments are disabled. Optional restaurant-registration links open an Instafresh website in your external browser. The links specify the market and do not include your locally stored profile, address or orders. The website may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and handles anything you submit there under its own privacy policy.

Retention, security and deletion

Demo records remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. In Android Settings, open Apps, select InstaFoods, choose Storage or Storage & cache, then choose Clear storage or Clear data. This deletes the local profile, addresses, order history, loyalty and preferences. Android backup is disabled for this build. Deleting app storage does not erase anything you submitted separately to an external website.

Privacy contact

For questions about this testing build or deleting your local data, contact the Instafresh team at instafreshadmin@gmail.com. You do not need to include your address or order history in your email.
